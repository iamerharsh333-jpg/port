What I build

I'm a Python developer and AI/ML engineer. Most of my work sits where messy data becomes something usable — ETL and validation workflows, REST APIs in Flask, Django and FastAPI, and applications powered by large language models through the OpenAI, Claude and Gemini APIs.

On the GenAI side I work with LangChain orchestration, retrieval-augmented generation, function calling and vector search. On the data side it's PySpark, Airflow and the unglamorous quality checks that stop a pipeline from lying to you.

Underneath both sits neural network work in TensorFlow — ANNs and CNNs applied to vision and time-series problems — and the linear algebra, probability and optimisation that make those models something I can reason about rather than just call.

A systems-testing background gave me a QA habit that never switched off: I look for the edge case before it reaches production. I'm comfortable owning a project end to end, from ingestion through to a deployed interface.
